# Recommended Grounding Set — PerShiaA Strategic Surface

A curated subset of `sources.md` designed to support **PerShiaA strategic analysis** of the Iran–Israel–US system. It is broad enough to expose competing narratives and system signals, but tight enough for repeated polling.

The grounding set is the **evidence surface**, not the analytical doctrine. Strategic interpretation is defined in `PERSHIAA-STRATEGIC-FRAMEWORK.md`.

## Design rules

- Primary sources anchor factual claims.
- Opposing framings are paired to expose divergence rather than averaged.
- OSINT tests physical plausibility, chronology, geolocation, and material claims.
- Economic, diplomatic, nuclear, maritime, and narrative sources are treated as first-class strategic signals.
- Think-tank sources broaden hypothesis space but do not override primary evidence.
- Every material assessment should retain a traceable path back to the evidence.

## How PerShiaA should use the set

1. **Establish evidence** — retrieve the primary claim or document where possible.
2. **Triangulate** — check independent, opposing, and OSINT evidence.
3. **Build the delta** — compare with the previous baseline.
4. **Map the system** — actor, theatre, objective, constraints, leverage.
5. **Test escalation** — identify thresholds and off-ramps.
6. **Test resilience** — determine whether strategic depth materially changed.
7. **Check second-order effects** — assess likely actor adaptation.
8. **Generate scenarios** — produce competing, bounded trajectories.
9. **Attach indicators** — define observable signals for confirmation or falsification.
10. **Record uncertainty** — preserve gaps instead of filling them with narrative.

## Polling guidance

- **Minutes during active kinetic windows:** IDF Spokesperson, Al-Masirah, Tasnim, primary official channels.
- **Hourly:** regional and wire sources.
- **Daily:** ISW/CTP, IAEA, UN, think tanks.
- **Event-driven:** economic, maritime, diplomatic, sanctions, and nuclear sources whenever a relevant signal appears.

The objective of polling is not maximum volume. It is **timely detection of material strategic change**.
